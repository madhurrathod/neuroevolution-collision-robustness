Controls

Driving

W or ↑Accelerate
S or ↓Brake. Once the car has stopped, keep holding to reverse
A D or ← →Steer left / right. Tap for a gentle turn; holding at speed builds up to full steering, and then the car skids
SpaceHandbrake. Locks the rear wheels: the car slows down for as long as you hold it (about half as hard as the brake) and the engine stops pulling. Together with A or D the tail slides out, so a short tap going into a bend makes the car drift. At a standstill it holds the car

Good to know

  • Grass is slow and slippery; how slow depends on your tyres.
  • Slow down for the yellow-and-black speed bumps: too fast and the car jumps and is damaged.
  • Damage makes the car slower. Repair it in the garage (not during a race). There is no reset: drive the car back onto the road yourself.
  • Races (stadium): before the start choose Sprint (5 laps), Championship (10), Endurance (20) or Custom (1–200). Wait on the grid for the three red lights to turn green. During a race the garage is closed; the menu has "Retire from the race".
  • In the stadium the outer wall is solid: hit it and you lose speed and scrape along it.
  • Pit stop (stadium): drive into the pit lane and stop in box 1, framed in yellow. The crew changes all four wheels; press 1–5 while the old wheels come off to choose the new tyres. You get the car back at GO.
